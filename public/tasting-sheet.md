### Michelin guide (0–3, with evidence)

Points: 0 no star · 1 ★ worth a stop · 2 ★★ worth a detour · 3 ★★★ worth the journey. On a first audit, consistency is scored only across the whole sample.

| Criterion | What is valued in the system | Steps | Points | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quality of ingredients | Tokens and assets with a single source, typed, and with origin | 0 · 1 | | |
| Mastery of techniques | Components faithful to their specs, accessible, and with all their states | 2 · 4 | | |
| Harmony of flavors | The same problem, solved the same way everywhere | 1 · 3 | | |
| Personality of the chef expressed in their cuisine | The brand is recognizable without seeing the logo | 0 · 3 | | |
| Consistency across the entire menu and over time | Across the entire sample and from one audit to the next | 1 · 6 | | |
